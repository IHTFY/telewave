How to play

⟵ ColdHot ⟶

Every card has two opposites. A hidden target sits somewhere between them. Give a clue that points at it, and your team moves the needle there.

On your turn

  1. 1

    Clue giver: hold Hold to peek to see the target. Everyone else looks away.

  2. 2

    Say a clue that fits that spot. For Cold / Hot, a lukewarm bath is near the middle.

  3. 3

    The team agrees, then drags the needle to their guess. Arrow keys work too.

  4. 4

    Press Guess to open the shade and score it.

Scoring

The closer to the centre, the more points. Anything outside the bands scores 0.

Bet. The other team can drop the gem on the left or right of the needle before the guess. Right side, 1 point. A bullseye cancels it.

4

Catch up. Score 4 while still behind and you go again.

10

Win. First to 10 wins. Team 2 starts on 1. A tie goes to sudden death.

Buttons

New card
Deal the next card. Pass the turn if the last one was scored.
Clear
Reset the needle and bet.
%
Show the needle as a number. Handy on a video call.
New game
Start over with scores at zero.

Tap a score slot or the -/+ buttons to fix a score. Tap the turn badge to switch teams, or a team name to rename it.

Two devices

Shuffle Enter the same word and card number on each device. Both show the same card and target, so the clue giver can peek on their own phone. Then everyone presses New card together.
